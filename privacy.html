Privacy

Max’s Picks is a static website. I do not ask you to create an account, and I do not run a comment form or a checkout on this site.

The site is meant to be hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested) as described in GitHub’s own privacy policy. I do not currently add a separate analytics script, advertising pixel, or tracking cookie of my own. If that changes, I will update this page.

Links to Amazon, Bookshop.org, Fanatics, NordVPN, Hostinger, and other third-party sites go to those companies’ websites. Once affiliate programs are approved, some of those links will include tracking codes so a commission can be credited. Those retailers have their own privacy policies and may set cookies when you visit them. I do not control that.

If you email me or message me on X, I will use that conversation to reply to you and not for a mailing list, unless you ask to be added to one.
