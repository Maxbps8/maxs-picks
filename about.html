I’m Max. I live in Villa Hills, Kentucky, just south of Cincinnati. This site is a set of short, plain lists — things I would actually tell a neighbor to check before they buy, and books I would put on a table and let someone pick up.

I drive a Tesla Model 3 Performance and a 2026 Model S Plaid, so the Tesla pages are written for people who own the cars, not for people who want a rental-host kit. I will not recommend car-sharing, Turo, or short-term rental tools here. That would compete with work I already do.

We have two large dogs, both around two and a half years old: Red, a ~55 lb female, and Snowball, a ~65 lb white male. One of them sheds a lot. The dog articles start from that household, not from a show-ring catalog.

I am also shopping for a two-person infrared sauna and a cold plunge that will fit a finished basement, and for a fire pit on a paver pad in the yard. Those pages are written as what I’m choosing and why. I have not bought the sauna, the plunge, or the fire pit yet, and I will not pretend I have.

I follow Kentucky Wildcats basketball first, and I also follow the Cincinnati Bengals and the Cincinnati Reds. The sports pages start with official UK gear and then the two Cincinnati teams. I like true crime as a reader — the book list in that genre is written in a sober voice, and the library is a fair first stop.

Where it is relevant, I prefer to point people toward conservative, Christian-owned, or plainly run businesses I can stand behind. I will not pretend a product is something it isn’t, and I will not invent a price, a rating, or a review I did not see.

This is not investment advice, and it is not a bourbon or whiskey guide. If those subjects ever appear later, it will be after I decide they belong here.

You can find me on X at @Maxbps8.
