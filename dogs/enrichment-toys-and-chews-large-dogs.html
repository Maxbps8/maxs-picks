We have two large dogs: Red, about 55 pounds, and Snowball, about 65 pounds. I bought the toys and chews named below. I am not a veterinarian, and I am not calling this a lab test. Supervise chew sessions, match the size to the dog, and confirm the live listing before you order.

The shedding, bed, crate, and harness pages cover the unglamorous gear. This one is about giving two busy dogs something to do that is not the couch. I bought a snuffle mat, a snuffle ball, a soccer-style interactive ball, and yak chews. Some of those listings warn they are not for aggressive chewers. That warning is why I still watch them, especially Snowball.

If you are still fighting hair, start with the shedding-gear article. This page will not replace a walk.

Snuffle mats and snuffle balls

A snuffle mat is a fabric puzzle you hide kibble or treats in so the dog has to forage. I bought a ZMUBB snuffle mat listed at about 31 by 19 inches and a Larimuer snuffle ball, 20 cm, in ocean blue. Those sizes were what the listings said when I ordered; measure the space you have, and wash the mat as the care label allows.

I like these because they slow the dogs down and do not require batteries. I do not leave a stuffed mat out as an all-day free-for-all if either dog starts to shred the fabric. If you want a similar mat, search snuffle mats for large dogs on Amazon and check the dimensions, whether it is machine-washable, and whether the seller shows a dog in this weight range.

A soccer-style interactive ball

I bought a Qraxond interactive soccer-style dog ball. The listing I used describes an automatic moving or jumping ball with straps, USB rechargeable, and it is explicit that it is not for aggressive chewers. I am not going to talk around that. For a 55-to-65-pound household, I treat it as a supervised toy on a clear floor, not as something to leave in a crate with a dog who likes to peel toys open. The maker also has a product page for the same style of ball.

If that exact listing is gone, search interactive dog soccer balls on Amazon and read the chew warning and the size range before you click buy.

Yak chews, in a size for a large dog

I bought EcoKind yak cheese chews sold for large dogs, in an 8+ chew pack and I have also ordered their smaller size at other times. The brand site is EcoKind Pet Treats. Yak chews are hard. They are not a toy to leave with an unsupervised dog, and a stub small enough to swallow is a reason to take it away. I am not a veterinarian; if your dog has dental issues or a sensitive stomach, ask yours before you copy my shopping list.

For dogs in the 55-to-65-pound range, buy a chew the listing actually describes for large dogs, not a puppy stick. If you need another pack, search EcoKind large-dog yak chews on Amazon and match the size on the package.

What I would skip

  • Anything the listing says is not for aggressive chewers, if your dog is one — unless you are willing to supervise and accept that it may not last.
  • Rawhide, if you have a reason to avoid it; I am using yak chews instead, not declaring a medical rule.
  • Buying five gadgets in one week instead of one toy the dogs will actually use, plus a walk.

Straight recommendation

In this house the enrichment kit I actually bought is a washable snuffle mat, a snuffle ball, a supervised interactive soccer ball, and large-dog yak chews. Measure the dog, read the chew warnings, and stay in the room until you know how they treat the thing. I will not invent a “percent less barking” number.
