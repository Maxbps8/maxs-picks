Buying fan gear for someone else is how you end up with a jersey in the wrong size or a name they would not have chosen. This guide starts with gifts that do not require a fitted-cap size or a jersey number, then works up to the items you should only buy if you actually know the details.

I will not quote a price or promise holiday shipping cutoffs. Check those on the live page the week you order.

If you do not know their size

Safe gifts:

  • An adjustable or snapback hat in the team they actually watch (not the one you wish they watched). Lids: team hats at Lids · Fanatics: team hats at Fanatics
  • A scarf, beanie, or winter hat in the right colors, if the season makes sense: team winter gear at Fanatics or winter fan gear at Rally House
  • A small licensed item — a mug, ornament, or similar — only if they are the kind of person who will use it. Gift aisles are full of things that become clutter: team gifts at Rally House or team gifts at Fanatics
  • A gift card from the official shop if you are truly guessing. Amazon gift cards pay no Associates commission, and I would still rather you give a useful card than a wrong jersey. Link to the retailer without pretending it is a money-maker: official shop gift card page (non-commission if it is a gift card).

If you know they wear a certain shirt size

A licensed tee or hoodie in that size is the workhorse gift.

  • Confirm whether they like a relaxed or fitted shirt, then use that brand’s chart: licensed team apparel at Fanatics · team apparel at Rally House · Homage team tees

If you know they want a jersey

Only then. You need: team, authentic vs replica, size (jerseys often fit differently from tees), and whether they want a current player, a blank, or a custom name. Spell the name. Recheck the number.

  • UK: Kentucky Wildcats jerseys at Fanatics
  • Bengals: Bengals jerseys at Fanatics
  • Reds: Reds jerseys at Fanatics

If you are even slightly unsure, buy the blank or do not buy the jersey.

Picking the team honestly

In this part of the country it is normal to have more than one. A UK household may still be a Bengals house on Sunday and a Reds house in the summer. If you do not know, ask, or pick the lower-risk item (hat, tee) in the team you have actually seen them wear.

Straight recommendation

Most good gifts in this category are a licensed hat or shirt from an official-leaning retailer, not a surprise authentic jersey. Spend the extra care on getting the team and the fit right, not on a bigger logo.
