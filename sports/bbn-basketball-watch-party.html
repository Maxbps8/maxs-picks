If you are having people over for a UK game, you need three things that actually work: a way to watch the game legally, enough seats and snacks, and a piece of official gear that will still be wearable in March. The merch rules are the same ones on the official vs. bootleg page and in the UK, Bengals, and Reds gift guide. This page is just narrower: basketball night, Kentucky first.

Watch the game on a legitimate stream or broadcast

I will not help anyone hunt a pirated stream. Use the conference or network that actually has the game that night — often ESPN or the SEC Network, or a service that carries them — and check the listing the day of the game. YouTube TV is one legal live-TV option I have looked at; whether it still carries the channel you need is a question for their current channel list, not for a paragraph I freeze here. The official UK Athletics site, UKAthletics.com, is where I check schedules and official announcements.

If a guest cannot follow a stream, a plain high-definition antenna for over-the-air games is still worth having as a backup when the local broadcast is available. I am not selling a TV model, and I will not invent a screen size.

Official UK gear people will actually wear

Start at the official team shop, then a licensed retailer you already know. The official University of Kentucky Athletics online shop is operated with Fanatics; UK Athletics announced a long-term Fanatics partnership in April 2026. You can also use Kentucky Wildcats gear at Fanatics, Kentucky hats at Lids, Rally House, and Homage for casual shirts. Look for the official marks, and if a price looks too cheap on a random storefront, read the bootleg checklist before you click.

  • A hat or a hoodie if you do not know someone’s jersey size: Wildcats hats at Lids · hoodies and tees at the UK team shop
  • A basketball tee or shooting shirt, sized from that brand’s chart, not from a memory of a 1990s Champion cut: licensed UK apparel at Fanatics
  • A jersey only if you know the size and whether they want a replica, a player name, or a blank: UK jerseys at the official shop · UK jerseys at Fanatics

I would rather hand someone a licensed hat that fits than a mystery “University of KY” shirt from a checkout they will not be able to return. Everyday hats and shirts are also on the hats and casual gear page.

The unglamorous watch-party list

  • Enough chairs that people are not standing in the doorway. I will not recommend a sofa for you.
  • A simple snack table. If you want a shared bowl or a pack of paper goods, search game-day serving bowls on Amazon or buy them locally. Licensed UK snack helmets are optional, and they are still a merch decision — use the official shop if you go that way.
  • A place for coats, and a plan if the game goes to overtime.

What I would skip

  • A “limited drop” jersey from a shop that will not say whether it is licensed.
  • Telling guests to use a shady stream.
  • Buying five novelty items instead of one good hat and a working TV input.

Straight recommendation

For a UK basketball night, get the game on a legitimate broadcast or live-TV service, put out chairs and snacks, and buy official Wildcats gear from the UK team shop, Fanatics, Lids, or another licensed retailer. Confirm the size and the official mark on the live page. I will not invent a score-night ritual or a price.
