Historical fiction — novels that make a past world feel lived in

Historical fiction is for readers who want a novel first and a history lesson second. The best of it still has to work as a story if you already know how the war ends. This list is part of the general fiction shelf on Max's Picks.

For each title, links go to the bookseller so you can check the current edition and price.

Wars and their aftermath

  • The Killer Angels — Michael Shaara. The Battle of Gettysburg, told from several commands. A classic of American historical fiction, and a better Civil War novel starting point than a random romance with a battlefield cover. Bookshop.org · Amazon · Audible · Libro.fm
  • All the Light We Cannot See — Anthony Doerr. Occupied France and a few carefully drawn lives in World War II. Very widely read; if the reader already finished it, try something else on this page rather than a second copy. Bookshop.org · Amazon · Audible · Libro.fm
  • The Nightingale — Kristin Hannah. Also World War II France, more in the popular-novel lane than Doerr. A frequent book-club gift. Bookshop.org · Amazon · Audible · Libro.fm
  • The Book Thief — Markus Zusak. Nazi Germany, narrated in a way that is its own device. Often handed to older teens and adults; it is not a cute children’s book. Bookshop.org · Amazon · Audible · Libro.fm

Other times, other places

  • Wolf Hall — Hilary Mantel. Thomas Cromwell and the court of Henry VIII. Dense, brilliant, and not a light first novel if the reader wants a fast plot. The trilogy continues with Bring Up the Bodies and The Mirror & the Light if they are hooked. Bookshop.org · Amazon · Audible · Libro.fm
  • Hamnet — Maggie O’Farrell. A novel around the death of Shakespeare’s son. Quiet and grief-struck; O’Farrell’s 2026 novel Land is on the year-end gift shortlist if you want something newer. Bookshop.org · Amazon · Audible · Libro.fm
  • The Pillars of the Earth — Ken Follett. A long cathedral-building saga in medieval England. For readers who like a big plot and a lot of pages. Bookshop.org · Amazon · Audible · Libro.fm
  • Pachinko — Min Jin Lee. A Korean family in Japan across the 20th century. If the reader then wants Lee’s 2026 follow-up, American Hagwon is on the year-end gift shortlist. Bookshop.org · Amazon · Audible · Libro.fm

How I would use this list

If you want a Civil War novel: Shaara. If you want a widely loved WWII novel: Doerr or Hannah, depending on whether the reader likes literary or book-club pacing. If you want something denser: Mantel. If you want a family saga: Lee.

Honest limits

This is a starter shortlist of widely known titles, not a claim that I have reread every page this year, and not a ranked “best of all time.” If Max has not read a book and is not comfortable recommending it under his name, it should be removed or relabeled before this page goes live.
