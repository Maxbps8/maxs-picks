Whodunits and mysteries — a shortlist for people who want a puzzle

This list is for readers who want a crime story with a question in it: who did it, how, or whether the detective has the right person. Thrillers that are mostly a chase are not the focus. True crime (nonfiction) has its own page on this site.

For each title, links go to the bookseller so you can check the current edition and price.

I am not listing a price. Confirm the edition (hardcover, paperback, translator, or narrator) on the day you buy.

Classics, if they have not read them

  • And Then There Were None — Agatha Christie. The engine of the closed-circle mystery. If they have already read it, Murder on the Orient Express or The Murder of Roger Ackroyd are the usual next Christies — confirm which one they have not already had spoiled. Bookshop.org · Amazon · Audible · Libro.fm
  • The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes — Arthur Conan Doyle (any decent complete or selected-stories edition; check the table of contents). Short cases, easy to pick up. A fine gift if you like the stories, not only the movies. Bookshop.org · Amazon · Audible · Libro.fm

Modern puzzles and detective novels

  • The Thursday Murder Club — Richard Osman. A light, talky English mystery. An easy gift and an easy audiobook. If they liked it, the series continues; do not accidentally buy book four first. Bookshop.org · Amazon · Audible · Libro.fm
  • In the Woods — Tana French. The first of the Dublin Murder Squad books. Moodier and more literary than Osman; some readers bounce off the ending. Start here only if they like atmosphere as much as a tidy solution. Bookshop.org · Amazon · Audible · Libro.fm
  • Magpie Murders — Anthony Horowitz. A mystery inside a mystery, from a writer who knows the Christie machinery and likes to show his work. Bookshop.org · Amazon · Audible · Libro.fm
  • The Cuckoo’s Calling — Robert Galbraith (J.K. Rowling). The first Strike novel. A contemporary detective story; if they want the series, start at book one. Bookshop.org · Amazon · Audible · Libro.fm
  • The Guest List — Lucy Foley. A wedding-on-an-island mystery. Popular, plotty, and more “whodunit weekend” than literary detective fiction. Bookshop.org · Amazon · Audible · Libro.fm

How I would use this list

If you want a safe gift: Christie or Osman. If you want a clever modern puzzle: Horowitz. If you want something darker and slower: French. Check whether they already own book one of a series before you wrap book one again.

Honest limits

This is a starter shortlist of widely known titles, not a claim that I have reread every page this year, and not a ranked “best of all time.” If Max has not read a book and is not comfortable recommending it under his name, it should be removed or relabeled before this page goes live.
